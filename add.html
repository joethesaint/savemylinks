What happens when you save

  1. The link is filed at once. You don't wait for the page to load.
  2. About a second later, SaveMyLinks reads the page's own title, picture and summary and adds them to the card. If you kept the suggested title, the page's own title replaces it.
  3. Find it again by typing any word of its title or your note, or by tapping its category.
  4. Save the same address twice and you're told it's already in your library, so nothing is duplicated.
SaveMyLinks keeps every link you save, filed by category, on your own server. This demo keeps them in your browser. Press / to search, ? for keyboard shortcuts.
