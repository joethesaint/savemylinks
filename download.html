Run your own SaveMyLinks.

One small program. Open it and your browser shows your own library: save any page, find it again by its title, your note or its category, with the page's real title, picture and summary. Your links stay in one file on your computer. No account, no setup.

First run

Windows

  1. Open savemylinks-windows-x64.exe.
  2. It isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen asks once: More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Your browser opens SaveMyLinks. Close the window to stop it.

macOS (Apple silicon)

  1. In Terminal: tar xzf savemylinks-macos-arm64.tar.gz && ./savemylinks-macos-arm64
  2. Blocked? Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway, run it again.

Linux

  1. tar xzf savemylinks-linux-x64.tar.gz && ./savemylinks-linux-x64
  2. Options: --port 8123, --db my.db, --no-browser.

Phone

  1. Open the live demo and choose Add to Home screen (Chrome: Install app).
  2. On Android it then appears in the Share menu: share a page to SaveMyLinks to save it.
